The Missing Piece

Making short form tactile

The biggest friction has been not knowing what we are talking about. This is the system that removes the Hail Mary.

CTAshare · save · comment

The rule of one

  • One core problem, one core promise, one outcome per video.
  • State the promise and the length up front: "In 60 seconds I'm gonna help you reduce your churn so you stop losing clients in the first 90 days."
  • Use "how I" over "how to". Disarming, less threatening.

Three content types

  • Demonstration — whiteboard + top down. "This, this, that." Visual frameworks and graphs.
  • Documentation — the gym pieces. Real client problems from the week, anonymised.
  • Teaching — concise, one takeaway, implementable now. Not mental masturbation they save and never action.

The daily capture

  • End of each day, voice note four questions:
  • What did they learn? What problem did I solve?
  • What did I teach? What do I do differently?
  • That alone is tons of content, and it keeps you calibrated.

The measurement

  • Log daily: Video · Hook · Topic · Likes · Avg view duration · Saves · Shares · Comments.
  • Watch saves — high saves can mean too dense to action now.
  • Track whether the people commenting tick the avatar box.
  • Monday review: best stat, highest watch, the topic.

Volume and shoot days

  • 14 short form a week (2/day) on top of the existing rhythm.
  • Shoot Mon / Wed / Fri half days. More frequency, more data.
  • 4 environments: this office, hallway, park, gym. Get 3 each, pick the best 14.
  • Pre production: write the hook + problem before the shoot.

The 30 day test

  • Throw a variety of formats and environments up. Gather data.
  • Isolate the new tactical content for ~2 weeks for clean signal. No collabs.
  • Then lock 4 content types for the next 60 days. Only test 1-2 new a month.
The Raw Material

Content angles, in your words

Pulled straight from the session. These bolt into the 14 videos and the cycles. A month of content sits in here already.

Churn · the inverse lever

  • My client lost $800,000 and never even knew it.
  • It feels like two steps forward and two back every month. Every month you start with less oxygen than the last. Who feels like that?
  • Anything above 3% churn? That's 60% of your business gone every year.
  • If you solved for net 0% churn, you could turn around one day and say I'm done signing clients. I'm good.

The math · reality check

  • Let's do the math of your business. (live, on the phone, be the logical guy while everyone else is the show pony.)
  • 100 clients, 15 minute check ins, and you've finally worked a 40 hour week. (the time on task takedown.)
  • The math can't account for the emotional toll. Add five hours. (data with empathy, not "do more.")

Scale without compromise

  • You can have your cake and eat it too. The art of coaching doesn't have to die at scale.
  • Most people think they have to compromise who they are to build something big. Not true.
  • Imagine if you didn't have to compromise.

Coaching is a career, not a hobby

  • If you're sick of being called just a PT, even though you're running over 10K, I finally worked out why.
  • Old way: coaching could be a hobby. New way: coaching is a career.
  • Your market ages up. You have to age up with it.

Impact equals income

  • Which business has more impact: 35 clients done brilliantly, or 350 who lose 10 kilos and move on? It's the bigger one. Fight me.
  • Income buys you impact. You're not changing the world coaching 25 people.
  • You have a duty to build something big if you're so impact driven.

I did it without influence

  • I did it without going viral.
  • I started the same way. Trading time for no money on the gym floor.
  • Come test me in person. You can't freeball in person.

Old way vs new way

  • Old way: just show up online. New way: treat your content as an ecosystem.
  • Old way: loom check ins. New way: written check ins. They can be done by anyone.
  • Being a good coach isn't enough. Business is a separate skill.

Profit vs revenue · the higher bracket

  • They chase 70-80K months and it resets to zero on the first.
  • Vertical growth feels fun until you don't have it, then you're back on the treadmill.
  • Revenue minus tax, minus GST, minus a lifestyle you've already adjusted to.

Been burned · scepticism

  • I'm a big fan of scepticism. Check for receipts.
  • If you've been burnt and you're scared to be burnt again, the only thing happening is you staying smaller.
  • Worst case you find more info and stay the same. Best case you're wrong and you get wealthier.
